02 · Identity

A mark of
connected progress.

Use the complete signature for corporate communication. Use the symbol when space is tight or recognition is already established.

Clear space

Keep one symbol-stroke of clear space around every side.

Minimum size

Digital: 32 px symbol / 140 px signature. Print: 12 mm / 36 mm.

Never

Do not stretch, rotate, recolour, add shadows or place on noisy imagery.

03 · Colour

Signal with
confidence.

Core ratio

Carbon / white 70% · Signal Blue 20% · Cyan 10%.

Accessibility

Target WCAG AA: 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text.

Print

Use colour-managed CMYK proofs before production.

05 · Voice

Expert.
Never distant.

We make technology understandable without making it simplistic. We sound composed, capable and close to the work.

↗

Clear

Prefer short words and active sentences.

✦

Useful

Answer “what does this change?” early.

●

Confident

Be specific. Avoid hype and empty superlatives.

↘

Human

Write to one person. Respect their time.

Write this

“Protect your team with practical controls and a clear response plan.”

Not this

“Leverage our best-in-class next-generation solutions to revolutionise your security posture.”
